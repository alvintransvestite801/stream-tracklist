## 💡 Troubleshooting common issues

Most users experience smooth operation, but issues occasionally happen. Follow these steps if the software stops or fails to identify a track.

### The software does not launch
Confirm that your antivirus software is not blocking the program. Sometimes security software identifies unknown programs as risks. Add an exception for the stream-tracklist folder in your antivirus settings.

### Songs remain unidentified
Audio quality significantly influences success rates. If a streamer speaks over the music or if there is excessive background noise, the software might struggle to identify the song. Use high-quality VODs where the music is clear and audible.

### Playlist does not appear in Spotify
Check your internet connection first. Open your Spotify app and confirm that you logged into the same account used during the setup process. Sometimes a short delay exists between the export and the arrival of the data in your Spotify library. Refresh your playlist tab.

### The program freezes during analysis
Large VODs require significant processing power. Avoid running high-demand tasks like video rendering or intense gaming while the program works. Close other unused applications to free up system memory and processor resources.

## 📂 Understanding the features

*   **Automatic Matching:** The system compares audio segments against international song databases to ensure accuracy.
*   **VOD Parsing:** The engine extracts audio data directly from your selected video link without requiring you to download the full video file first.
*   **Playlist Sync:** Changes made within the app reflect in your cloud-based Spotify library in real-time.
*   **Custom Naming:** Rename your playlists automatically based on the stream title or the date of the broadcast.
*   **Metadata Extraction:** The app attempts to pull artist names, track titles, and album art for every matched song.

## 🛡️ Privacy and safety

It does not store your Spotify password or share your personal information with third parties. All communication happens over encrypted channels to keep your data secure during the transfer process.
